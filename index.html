Straight answers

Before you open another tab.

A few practical answers about spreadsheet browsing, product details, and the links on this page.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize shopping links and notes. A useful list can include product categories, source links, prices, sizing information, and photo references. The name alone does not guarantee product quality, availability, or platform compatibility.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page is a category-based browsing guide. Its shopping links open CAFANS or the listed CAFANS category pages; it does not provide a downloadable spreadsheet or a live Oopbuy inventory feed.

How should I compare spreadsheet finds?

Compare the exact item, available size chart, material description, color options, product photos, and estimated total cost. Keep missing information marked as unknown rather than treating it as confirmed.

Do brand names or photos prove authenticity?

No. Brand names and reference photos do not authenticate a listing. Check the seller’s documentation, applicable brand authorization, and return terms. Purchase from authorized retailers when authenticity is essential.

Is CAFANS the official Oopbuy website?

This page is an independent CAFANS browsing guide and does not claim official affiliation with Oopbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, or Hermès.

What should I check before choosing a clothing size?

Compare the listing’s measurements with a garment that fits you. Check the stated measurement units, chest width, length, waist, and inseam as relevant. Do not assume that the same size label means the same fit across sellers.
